ART OF APPRECIATION

The Four-Step Tasting Ritual

True indulgence is a mindful sensory ceremony. Allow Velora to awaken every perception.

I

The Visual Sheen

Observe the satin gloss on the bar’s face, a testament to disciplined tempering at 31.5°C.

II

The Acoustic Snap

Break the bar close to your ear. A clean, crisp fracture confirms optimal cocoa crystal lattice.

III

The Terroir Bouquet

Inhale gently. Warm volcanic soils, sun-dried pods, and subtle nuances of wild orchid emerge.

IV

The 34°C Melt

Rest a piece on the tongue without chewing. Natural body heat dissolves the conched cocoa butter in silky harmony.
